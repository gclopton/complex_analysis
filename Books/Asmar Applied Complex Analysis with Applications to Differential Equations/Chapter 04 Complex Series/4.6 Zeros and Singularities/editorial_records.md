> [!theorem] Order and Local Factorization of a Zero of an Analytic Function
> Suppose that $f$ is analytic on an open set $U, z_{0}$ is in $U$, and $f\left(z_{0}\right)=0$. Then exactly one of the following holds:

> [!definition] Removable Singularities, Poles, and Essential Singularities
> An isolated singularity at $z_0$ means that $f$ is analytic in some punctured disk $0<|z-z_0|<R$ with $R>0$.

> Suppose that $z_{0}$ is an isolated singularity of $f$. Then
