### Before

````text
> [!definition] Robin boundary condition for a scalar field
> A Robin boundary condition is a linear relation between a scalar field and its outward normal derivative on the boundary:
> $$
> \alpha u+\beta\frac{\partial u}{\partial n}=h.
> $$
> The prescribed coefficients $\alpha,\beta$ are not both zero. In one dimension it may be written $c_1u(x_0,t)+c_2u_x(x_0,t)=h(t)$, with the endpoint orientation absorbed into $c_2$.
````
### After

````text
> [!definition] Robin boundary condition for a scalar field
> Robin conditions are linear boundary conditions of the form $c_{1} u\left(x_{0}, t\right)+c_{2} u_{x}\left(x_{0}, t\right)=h(t)$. The prescribed coefficients $c_1,c_2$ are not both zero.
````
